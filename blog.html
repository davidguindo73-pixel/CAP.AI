Guides on prompting, visual direction and responsible creative tools.

Guide

Write prompts that are easier to direct

Identify the subject, action, location, mood and key details. Instead of vague adjectives, describe what the camera sees. Start with “a cyclist crossing a wet city street at night,” then add “direct-flash street photography, reflections on asphalt, candid framing, 35mm grain, subject sharply focused.” Finally state what to avoid. Change one instruction at a time.

Motion

What a useful video prompt needs

Describe the subject's movement, the camera's movement, timing and details that should remain consistent. “The camera slowly tracks beside the runner” is more direct than “make it dynamic.” CAP.AI drafts instructions; real video generation is not connected yet.

Trust

Be clear about creative AI limits

A polished interface should not make a mockup look like a model output. Before integrations are added, explain quality, costs, usage limits and how files are handled. Use reference material you have permission to use and review results before publishing.
