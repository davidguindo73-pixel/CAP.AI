Current service

CAP.AI provides a prompt workspace and a prototype that can submit image/video generation jobs through a separately deployed API gateway to a third-party model provider. Generation is available only after the backend, credentials and a provider account with access and credits are configured. CAP.AI does not currently train or own its own foundation image/video model. Secure user accounts and subscriptions are not active.

Acceptable use

Do not use this service to plan unlawful activity, harassment, non-consensual intimate imagery, deceptive impersonation or infringement. Use references only when you have permission or another valid legal basis.

Third-party tools

Generation requests, and any reference images or videos you choose to upload, are sent to the third-party provider. Its terms, safety restrictions, pricing and privacy policy apply. Provider charges may be incurred through the configured provider account. CAP.AI cannot guarantee output accuracy, uniqueness, availability, licensing or suitability. You are responsible for your prompts, reference rights and reviewing outputs before using them.

Availability and future payments

Local drafts can be lost when browser data is cleared. CAP.AI currently has no paid CAP.AI plan. However, generation through your provider account can consume that provider’s credits or balance. Review model pricing before submitting jobs. Any future CAP.AI paid services need clear prices, renewal, refund, usage and cancellation terms before purchase.
