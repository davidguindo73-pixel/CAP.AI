Neon night street

A moody vertical short with wet pavement, distant traffic, direct flash and a slow handheld camera move.

A young adult in an oversized black hoodie walks through a neon-lit city street at 1 AM after rain. Purple and green lights reflect on the pavement; subtle handheld shake, direct flash, natural texture, film grain and consistent clothing. Start behind the subject, move slowly around the silhouette, then finish on the reflections.
