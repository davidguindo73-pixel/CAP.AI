About CAP.AI

Creative tools should feel like a studio, not a maze.

CAP.AI is an early-stage creative technology project focused on making image, video, and advertising workflows clearer and more approachable.

The person behind the platform

David Guindo.

I’m David Guindo, a computer engineering student interested in building technology that makes creative tools easier to use.

I started CAP.AI around a simple idea: turning an idea into a polished visual should feel approachable—not like you need a huge budget, complicated software, or years of production experience. I want to bring creative workflows together in one clear workspace for creators, students, and small businesses.

The mission

Make creating feel accessible.

The project is meant to reduce friction between having a creative idea and knowing how to communicate it to a media-generation tool.

The approach

Build with clarity.

CAP.AI brings prompts, visual direction, reference-guided workflows and advertising briefs into one place, with the intention of keeping complex tools easier to understand.

What works today

The website includes a Prompt Lab, an examples gallery, and a Create AI interface with a prototype integration to a separate generation API. Actual rendering requires the backend to be deployed and connected to a provider account with access and credits.

What is still in progress

CAP.AI does not currently train or own its own foundation model. Secure user accounts, cloud project storage, billing, persistent job queues and production-grade moderation still need to be built before a broad public launch.

Transparency matters. Some of the gallery items are reference images or illustrative concepts. They are not presented as media generated by CAP.AI.
