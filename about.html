About CAP.AI

Creative tools should feel like a studio, not a maze.

CAP.AI is a project to bring creative workflows into one clear workspace: describe an idea, direct the style and organize prompts.

What exists today

Landing page, showcase previews, prompt building, local reference preview, saved prompts and local profile.

What comes next

Real image/video models, secure accounts, cloud projects and payments.

Honest by design

Gallery tiles are visual mockups. The studio prepares prompts rather than generating media. Planned integrations are not presented as working AI features.
