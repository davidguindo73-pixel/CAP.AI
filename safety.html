Safety

Creative freedom with clear boundaries.

Starting principles for image, video and editing workflows.

Consent and identity

Do not create deceptive sexual or humiliating depictions of real people without consent, impersonate their voice/face or falsely imply endorsement.

Rights and references

Use images, footage, music and brand assets only with permission or another valid legal basis.

Lawful use

Do not use the platform to facilitate violence, exploitation, fraud, harassment or unlawful surveillance.

Review before sharing

Review outputs for inaccuracies, private information, stereotypes and misleading realism before publishing.

Current limit

This preview builds prompts; no model generation or automated moderation is connected. These principles do not imply production safeguards already exist.

Use Contact to prepare feedback and avoid sensitive information in public issues.
